# EV Share Admin Dashboard – Current Status

## Current Overview

The Admin Dashboard provides a broad set of tools for commerce, fleet operations, finance, and platform configuration. Core screens and actions are implemented, but several management workflows and live operational integrations still need completion or validation.

## Completed / Available

- **Dashboard and monitoring:** Platform, revenue, and fleet summaries; trip history and an active-trip map.
- **Store and orders:** Category and product management; order status, receipt, refund-resolution, and signed operating-contract review.
- **Users and approvals:** Sign-in, user listing and creation, role and permission management, registration and payment-request decisions, complaint responses, and consultation tracking.
- **Shipping and logistics:** Shipment creation and status management, carrier configuration, and delivery-area setup.
- **Fleet and rides:** Management of existing operating companies, vehicle settings and zones, vehicle and lock commands, and active-trip end or cancellation actions.
- **Finance and payments:** Wallet and transaction views, payment-method configuration, commission settings, VAT settlement entry, and payment event logs.
- **Content and settings:** Promotions, subscription discounts, slides, static pages, app versions, feature flags, cities, and general platform settings.

## Remaining / Needs Completion

- Complete user editing, suspension, deletion, and role-change controls; add document review and rejection reasons to registration decisions.
- Add admin creation flows for operating companies and vehicles, and complete commission-setting creation.
- Ensure active-trip monitoring covers the full live fleet and improve selection of orders and locks in larger datasets.
- Correct dashboard trend presentation and complete VAT navigation and management reporting or export needs.
- Resolve country-specific phone and currency inconsistencies, strengthen session recovery, and complete final end-to-end QA and release checks.

## Needs Verification

- Live data persistence and synchronization across the Admin Dashboard, Merchant, and Rider systems.
- Real outcomes of payment, refund, carrier, vehicle-device, and trip actions, including error recovery.
- Backend permission enforcement and the downstream effect of pricing, content, feature flags, and app settings.

## Current Management Summary

Admins can oversee commerce, users, shipments, fleet activity, trips, financial records, and platform settings, with actions available for many routine decisions. Full operational readiness depends on completing the identified management controls and proving the connected business and device flows with realistic data.
